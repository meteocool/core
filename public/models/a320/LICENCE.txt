Changes: converted from the published model into the format the flight
loads (scripts/models/convert-aircraft.py): scaled to the aircraft's true
size, turned into its own axes, meshes of one material joined, vertices
packed, textures re-encoded as WebP.

The landing gear is made to retract: its legs and doors are kept as parts
of their own, each turning on the hinge of the model's own rig, and a plain
grey well is added behind each belly door, where the model is open into the
cabin.

The wings' flaps, slats and spoilers and the fairings under the flaps, which
the model has as meshes of their own, are kept as parts too, each given a
hinge along its own leading edge to slide and turn on.

The ailerons, the elevators and the rudder, which the model has as shells of
the hull's mesh, are taken out of it as parts too
(scripts/models/control_surfaces.py), each turning about the middle of its
own rounded nose. No triangle is changed.

The flight deck's controls are made to move the same way
(scripts/models/cockpit_controls.py): the captain's sidestick, the speed
brake's lever, the landing gear's lever, both pairs of rudder pedals, the
parking brake's handle and the rudder trim's knob are taken out of the
cockpit's meshes as parts, each turning about a pivot of its own (the
pedals sliding). The thrust levers and the flap lever, which the model has
slots for but not levers, are added: plain grey blocks, a shaft and a
handle each.

The outside is repainted: the airline's livery, titles and marks removed and
the hull left white; "meteowings" titles along both sides; the meteocool logo
on both sides of the fin and of each engine, in place of the engine maker's
name; and stripes in the radar's colours up the rear fuselage and the fin,
round each engine's intake and up each wingtip. A few small parts whose
texture was a stretch of the hull's painted elsewhere are drawn in the colour
of the hull round them. Inside, nothing else is changed.
