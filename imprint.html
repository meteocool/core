Data sources and licences

meteocool processes everything it shows. Radar scans are composited from each radar's lowest elevation, reprojected, filtered for clutter and coloured; forecasts are extrapolated by meteocool. None of the providers below endorses meteocool, and all of them provide their data without warranty.

Radar

  • Germany: © Deutscher Wetterdienst (DWD), CC BY 4.0, modified. Also the source of the storm cells, the 3D volumes and the weather model maps.
  • Switzerland: © MeteoSwiss, CC BY 4.0, modified.
  • France: © Météo-France, Licence Ouverte 2.0 (Etalab), modified. The map shows each frame's time.
  • Czechia: © Český hydrometeorologický ústav (ČHMÚ), CC BY 4.0, modified.
  • Poland: The source of data is the Institute of Meteorology and Water Management – National Research Institute (IMGW-PIB). Data from IMGW-PIB has been processed by meteocool. CC BY 4.0.

Switzerland, France, Czechia and Poland arrive through EUMETNET's Open Radar Data.

Other data

  • Radar, United States: NOAA / National Weather Service, public domain.
  • Lightning: © Blitzortung.org and its contributors.
  • Model comparison: Open-Meteo.com, CC BY 4.0, from the national weather services it names.
  • Satellite imagery: contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data, processed by © OroraTech.
  • Base map: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL; tiles by Protomaps.
  • Terrain of the 3D map: © Mapterhorn, built from open elevation datasets, many of them CC BY 4.0. Mapterhorn's attribution page (as JSON) names every dataset and its licence.

Colour maps

  • StepSeq, Homeyer and Lang: Py-ART's StepSeq25, HomeyerRainbow and LangRainbow12, © 2013 UChicago Argonne, LLC, BSD 3-Clause (Py-ART, now maintained in cmweather); resampled for meteocool. The licence text is under Open-source software.
  • Viridis: from matplotlib, by Nathaniel J. Smith, Stéfan van der Walt and Eric Firing, CC0.
  • NWS: NOAA / National Weather Service's reflectivity palette, public domain.

Icons and artwork

  • Legend icons by Vitaly Gorbachev from www.flaticon.com.
  • Icons and adapted artwork (logo, iOS onboarding) by Freepik from www.flaticon.com.
  • Icons by Font Awesome (Fonticons, Inc.), CC BY 4.0, and Bootstrap Icons (The Bootstrap Authors), MIT.
