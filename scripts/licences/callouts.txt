The cockpit callouts in meteoair are spoken by the voice "am_fenrir" of
Kokoro-82M by hexgrad (https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M), licensed
under the Apache License, Version 2.0
(https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).

Kokoro-82M was trained on audio that includes:
- Koniwa (https://github.com/koniwa/koniwa), "tnc", licensed under CC BY 3.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)
- SIWIS French Speech Synthesis Database
  (https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/2353), licensed under CC BY 4.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
